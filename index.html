About Floating Analog Clock

Floating Analog Clock is a lightweight Chrome extension designed to keep time visible while you browse, work, study, or focus on important tasks. The clock stays on your screen as a floating overlay and provides a clean, distraction-free experience.

Built with simplicity in mind, the extension offers customization features while maintaining minimal resource usage.

Features

🕒 Floating Clock

Keep a beautiful analog clock visible while browsing.

🖱 Draggable

Move the clock anywhere on your screen with drag-and-drop support.

🎨 Color Adjustable

Customize colors and personalize the clock appearance.

🌍 All Language Support

Works smoothly across different languages and regions.

➖ Minimizable

Minimize and restore the clock whenever you need more space.

⚡ Lightweight

Designed to be fast and use minimal browser resources.

Why Use Floating Analog Clock?

Many clock extensions include unnecessary features and distractions. Floating Analog Clock focuses on what matters:

  • Clean and minimal design
  • Easy to use interface
  • Customization options
  • Smooth user experience
  • Low resource usage
  • Productivity-friendly

Privacy

Floating Analog Clock does not collect personal information, browsing history, or webpage content. Only user settings such as color, opacity, and position are stored locally for a consistent experience.
